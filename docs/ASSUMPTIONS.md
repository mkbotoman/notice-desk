# Assumptions

One line each, tagged with the workstream ID, e.g. `- [WS-1B] ...`. Feeds the short note.

- [M0] Business days exclude weekends and US federal holidays only. The holiday list is one config file; California dates can be added later.
- [M0] A cure period starts the day after the notice is sent (day 1 is the next day).
- [M0] "Due this week" is a rolling 7 days from asOf. The weekly summary keeps its own Mon–Fri windows.
- [M0] The default asOf is the data export date, with today one click away.
- [M0] Asset managers can add updates and hand cases back to Legal; property managers are read-only.
- [M0] An unparseable cure period becomes `unknown` (shown as "deadline missing"), distinct from `none`.
- [M0] Personas are not security. The seed ships to the browser and `?as=` can switch persona, so `can()` shapes the UI only. Deployment Protection is the access control.
- [M0] The deployed site sits behind a shared password (`ACCESS_PASSWORD` in Vercel env, enforced by `src/proxy.ts`), because reviewers won't have Vercel accounts. It is a gate, not user auth: one shared secret, no rate limiting, and the signed-in cookie lasts 7 days. Build assets under `/_next/static` stay public and contain no seed data. With no password set, production returns 503.
- [M0] The PII scan checks source, seed and rendered HTML/RSC payloads for dashed and bare nine-digit SSN shapes, but built JS and source maps only for the dashed form. Minified code contains bare nine-digit runs inside lookup tables, so the bare form there is noise. WS-5B should add an exact-value check against the local raw SSNs.
- [WS-1A] The default asOf is 2026-10-13, the latest date anywhere in the data (`date_sent` and `updates`).
- [WS-1A] A cure period such as "10 days" with no unit means calendar days; the data also contains "10 business days" side by side. 14 notices are affected.
- [WS-1A] `Breach` maps to `other_lease_term`, `AM` to the asset-management team, `0-30` to `1_30`, `Current` lease status to `active`, `Current` COI status to `valid`.
- [WS-1A] A text `amount_owed` ("Pending") becomes null and is flagged. `legal_fees` is lease-provision text and is kept verbatim as `legalFeesTerms`; `lender_notice` is a three-way enum; `status` is kept verbatim as `workflowStatus`.
- [WS-1A] COI status is stored as exported; the effective status (expired / expiring / valid) is computed from `expiresOn` at the current asOf, so moving the asOf control stays consistent. `Non-Compliant` is its own status, not expiry.
- [WS-1A] A lease with no row in the COI tool gets a synthesized `missing` COI record and counts as an insurance signal on the watchlist (decided by Maureen).
- [WS-1A] Leases pointing at properties that don't exist are kept with a null property, flagged, and shown under "Unknown property" (decided by Maureen). They are visible only to personas with all-property scope.
- [WS-1A] DQ-AR infers only a floor: the best aging the balance allows. It flags understated aging (error) and a zero balance with non-current aging (stale, warning). Overstated aging is not flagged, because partial payments lower the balance. Real data: zero flags.
- [WS-1A] A second lease on the same property and suite is flagged (rule DQ-SUITE, warning); both leases are kept.
- [WS-1B] A cure deadline is only "overdue" or "due this week" while the case is in Notice to Cure. In Default and Collections the cure period has already ended by definition, so those show the deadline as history and carry no overdue flag.
- [WS-1C] Pages live under `src/app/(shell)/` (not directly under `src/app/` as the plan's layout shows) so they share the sidebar and header; `/gate` stays outside it with no chrome.
- [WS-1C] Persona ids are `maya`, `founder`, `am-<name>` and `pm-<name>`. The persona choice and any changed as-of date are saved in the browser. A saved as-of date is ignored if the data date has changed since.
- [WS-1C] Nav items are gated by `can()` on one action each (Waiting on me needs `add_update`; Watchlist and Weekly summary follow `view_analytics`), never by persona kind. Typing a URL for a page the persona can't use shows a plain explanation instead of the page.
- [WS-1C] The home page is an overview for now. Asset managers land on "Waiting on me" once that page exists (M2).
- [WS-2A] The case list groups cases as Overdue, Due this week ("through" the as-of date plus 7), Later, Deadline missing, No cure deadline, and Cured and closed. A Default or Collections case that has a send date shows that date greyed with "Cure period ended"; it is never overdue.
- [WS-2A] The four stat cards are quick filters (the URL param `due` is `overdue` or `week`). They count the persona's open cases and ignore the other filters. Search matches tenant and property names only. Stage options list the four open stages, plus cured and closed once "Include closed" is on. The property filter is a checkbox list that does not close on an outside click. On phones the table scrolls inside its own box rather than turning into cards.
- [WS-2A] The mock's "Default since Oct 9" and "Opened Oct 26" sublines are not shown, because no case has such a date.
- [WS-2B] The stage stepper shows every step before the current one as done, even when a case skipped it (for example Cured straight from Notice to Cure); Cured and Closed share the last step. Notes may be added to finished cases; owner changes and stage moves may not. Legal's owner button hands the case to the other team only; an asset manager sees "Hand back to Legal" only on a case that is with Asset Management.
- [WS-2B] Case detail shows "Legal fees" as the lease's own wording (for example "Yes, Section 18.4"), not a dollar figure, because the data has no amount. Stage buttons come from the allowed transitions rather than the mock's fixed "Mark cured / Move to Default". A case outside the persona's scope shows a generic explanation with no tenant details.
- [WS-2B/2C] Dynamic routes list their known ids with `generateStaticParams`, because the shell's nav reads the current path, which blocks prerendering of a runtime-only parameter. Any other id renders on first request.
- [WS-2C] "Open cases" for a tenant means non-terminal cases the persona may view; "Total AR" sums the live balance over the persona's in-scope leases (credits included, null as 0). The history timeline covers only in-scope cases and skips notification events. An unknown tenant and an out-of-scope tenant show the same not-found panel. The mock's AR bar chart and "Cases, last 12 months" are left out: the data has no monthly history.
- [WS-2D] "Hand back to Legal" appears only on cases not already with Legal. A lease with several open cases shows its most urgent one. The Founder sees every open case in the inbox, read-only (permission `view_inbox`, granted to Legal, asset managers and the Founder). Property managers have no inbox; their home page (the overview) carries the "In trouble" list instead. Everyone lands on the overview. Mock items with no data behind them are listed in `docs/NEXT_STEPS.md`.
- [WS-2D] "In trouble" lists leases. An open case that has no lease (an orphan notice) still appears in the case list and the waiting list but not there; the real data has none.
- [WS-2C] The "Also appears as" name variants are shown only to all-property personas, because a variant can come from a lease at a property a scoped persona can't see.
- [WS-3A] "Within 3 business days" counts business days after the as-of date up to and including the deadline; a deadline on the as-of date is "approaching". Deadline-passed notices always go to Legal; deadline-approaching ones go to the case owner (the property's asset manager when the case is with Asset Management). Stage-change drafts go to the property's asset manager, owner-change drafts to the new owner's team. Only events recorded in the app drive these (imported notes never do).
- [WS-3A] Insurance drafts attach to the lease's open case, or else the tenant's first case, because a notification needs a case; a certificate for a tenant with no case at all gets no draft. A change in the as-of date that turns "expiring" into "expired" raises a new draft. Dismissing a draft is not logged as an event (no event type exists) and has no stored time.
- [WS-3A] The app raises drafts on its own after load, for any persona (logged as the actor "Notice Desk (automatic)"). On the real data that is about 22 drafts on first load. Editing an automatic draft's text makes its source "manual". Nothing is emailed; the screen says so.
- [WS-3B] Analytics shows only what the data supports: no stage-over-time, days-in-stage, time-to-resolution or AR history. "Sent" is `dateSent`, falling back to the `case_opened` event date. AR is each lease's live balance counted once, credits netted. Cure rate is cured divided by cured plus closed (4 of 7). App activity counts notes, stage changes and owner changes by the week they were recorded.
- [WS-3C] The watchlist lists leases in the persona's scope with no open case and at least one signal (rent 31–60 days or worse by the worse of recorded and implied aging, insurance expired / non-compliant / missing, or a listed reason). Leases whose only signals are listed reasons sit in "Listed risks". A lease with only closed or cured cases counts as having no open case. "Open case" passes every signal, listed ones included, into the new case's opening note. Data-quality filters are local state, not URL parameters.
- [WS-3C] On the data-quality page, info-level entries count as "routine repairs" and warnings and errors as "flagged for review". DQ-PROP entries are recorded with action "flagged", so the "Fixed" filter excludes them.
- [WS-3D] The weekly summary covers the 7 days ending on the as-of date, both ends included. New cases are those whose send date falls in the window plus those opened in the app. A stage change counts on its effective date. Live AR is the signed sum of lease balances over open cases in view (a lease counted once); there is no "AR movement" because there is no history. The HTML copy uses inline styles because email clients ignore stylesheets.
